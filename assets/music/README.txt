WATA SPO background music
=========================

Drop your music files into this folder with these exact names.
Each can be .mp3 or .ogg. Any file that is missing is simply silent.

  home.mp3      home screen and most menus (loops)
  gacha.mp3     Recruit screen (loops)
  battle.mp3    normal battles (loops)
  boss.mp3      any boss without its own theme (loops)
  boss-phantom.mp3   Crimson Phantom fights (loops)
  boss-core.mp3      Glitch Core fights (loops)
  boss-final.mp3     The Algorithm, the final boss (loops)
  story.mp3     story scenes and off-stream talks (loops)
  event.mp3     the Live screen: events, Ranked Raid, Daily Stream (loops)
  victory.mp3   short jingle after winning a battle (plays once)

The game fades between tracks automatically. Players can change the
music volume, or turn music off, in Settings.

Only use music you have the rights to use (your own, or tracks whose
licence allows use in a free fan game). Credit the composer in Settings
> About if the licence asks for it.
